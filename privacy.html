Privacy Policy

URL Manager (UM) · Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what URL Manager ("the app") does with your information. In short: your saved links and thumbnails stay on your device and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Information the app stores

  • Your saved links (the URLs you add), saved on your device.
  • Thumbnail previews: small images for your saved links, plus lists of which links have no preview, saved on your device.
  • A Google sign-in token, only if you connect Google Drive. It is saved on your device so the app can sync without asking you to sign in every time.

The app has no server of its own, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no ads. The developer never receives any of this information.

How thumbnails are made

To show a preview, the app visits the web pages of the links you saved and downloads their preview images, directly from your device, the same way a web browser would. Those websites can see that request, as with any page you open. On Android, if you set it up yourself, the app can instead ask a helper program that you run on your own computer on your home network to do this.

Google user data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, the app asks for one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: see, create and edit only the Google Drive files that this app created.

What the app accesses: only the "URLManager" folder it creates in your Google Drive and the files inside it (your saved links and thumbnail previews). It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

How it is used: to upload your links and thumbnails to that folder and download changes made on your other devices, so they stay the same everywhere. That is the only use.

Where it is stored: on your device and in your own Google Drive. Data goes directly between your device and Google; it does not pass through any other server.

Sharing: Google user data is never shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

URL Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect: tap Drive → Disconnect in the app. This removes the sign-in token from that device. Your links stay on the device.
  • Remove access completely: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove URL Manager.
  • Delete synced files: delete the "URLManager" folder from your Google Drive.
  • Delete everything on a device: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
