URL Manager (UM)

A personal link saver for Android and Windows, with thumbnail previews.

What it does

  • Save links: paste one or many URLs at once. Duplicates are skipped.
  • Find links: filter by website domain or by words in the link.
  • Thumbnail previews: shows a preview image for each saved link.
  • Edit, remove and copy saved links.

Syncing with Google Drive

If you choose to connect a Google account, URL Manager keeps your saved links and thumbnail previews the same on all your devices by saving them to a folder called URLManager in your own Google Drive.

The app uses Google's drive.file permission. That means it can only see and change the files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.

Connecting Google Drive is optional. Without it, the app works normally and keeps everything on your device only.

Privacy
